# Garran — Appearance Specifications

**Character:** Garran  
**Role:** Village blacksmith  
**Age:** Approximately 55  
**Art style:** Dark graphic novel — textured ink linework, crosshatching, muted earthy tones  
**Visual authority:** `master-sheet.png` (user-selected reference; use as the primary visual source for future artwork)  
**Status:** Reference design approved by user; any new view or pose must be checked against the master sheet.

## Core appearance

## Standard work clothing

- Light, coarse linen shirt, worn and stained, sleeves rolled to the forearms.
- Dark, heavy leather smith's apron with extensive rust-colored and soot-colored wear.
- Wide leather belt and straps, with practical metalworking tools at the waist.
- Loose, dark work trousers and substantial scuffed boots.
- Practical, visibly used materials: no polished armor, elaborate jewelry or decorative fantasy costume.

## Expression guidance

- **Neutral:** serious, tired, observant; brows naturally heavy.
- **Stern:** tighter mouth, deeper brow crease, without changing bone structure.
- **Amused:** small, imperfect smile; not a charming or polished grin.
- **Concerned:** tension around eyes and mouth; believable restraint.
- **Angry:** intense expression, but no facial redesign or exaggerated villain styling.
- **Thoughtful:** focused, guarded, one hand may rest near beard.

## Identity lock — required for future renders

1. **Always provide `master-sheet.png` as a visual reference.** Do not regenerate Garran from this description alone.
2. Preserve the **receding hairline**, unkempt swept-back waves, substantial white/gray hair, and **mostly white full beard**. Earlier alternate sheets depicting a younger man with a tidy bun are **not canonical**.
3. Preserve the same brow, nose, facial width, cheek scar, eye placement, beard outline, age and body proportions in every angle.
4. Avoid idealizing him: no chiseled jaw, smooth skin, symmetrical model face, exaggerated heroic musculature, or glamorous styling.
5. Keep his blacksmith's apron, shirt, boots and tools consistent unless a specific scene requires an outfit change.
6. Do not invent permanent scars, tattoos, jewelry or other identifying features without approval.
7. For each new full-body, dialogue, icon or expression image, compare the result against the reference sheet before accepting it. AI generation may still drift and requires review.
8. Maintain the game's grounded medieval dark-graphic-novel visual language; no photorealistic or cartoon reinterpretations.
